YEAR WHEEL - START HERE

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you can find it again.
2. Open yearwheel.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
   (right-click > Open With > Google Chrome). Safari and Firefox also work,
   but save changes as new copies in your Downloads folder.
3. Click "Open" and choose demo-department-2026-27.json to see a demo wheel
   (all events are fictional), or click "New" to start your own from a template.
4. Click "Save" to keep your wheel as a .json file. That file is your wheel.
5. To add many events from a spreadsheet, start from
   year-wheel-import-template.csv and use Import > Spreadsheet (.csv).

Nothing is installed and nothing is sent over the internet.
The full guide is in Year-Wheel-User-Guide.pdf.
